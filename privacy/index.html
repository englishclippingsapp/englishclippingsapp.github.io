Privacy Policy

Effective 14 September 2026

English Clippings is designed to help you save and study useful English. This policy explains what information the app handles and when information may be sent to another service.

Information stored on your device

The app stores your clippings, highlighted words, notes, customized translations, learning progress, garden preferences, vocabulary settings, and translation cache on your device. You can remove this information using the reset and deletion controls in the app.

Photos selected or captured for text recognition are processed on the device to extract text. English Clippings does not intentionally upload or retain those photos.

Translation

Translation is processed on your device using Apple Translation and the language packages managed by iOS. Apple's handling of its translation technology is subject to Apple's privacy policy and service terms.

Dictionary lookups

When you request a definition, the highlighted word is sent to EnglishDictionaryAPI.com. The surrounding clipping is used locally to help choose the relevant dictionary information and is not intentionally included in that dictionary request.

Sign in with Apple

Sign in with Apple is optional. If you sign in, the app may store the Apple account identifier and profile information Apple makes available, such as your name or relay email address, in the iOS Keychain. Apple manages the authentication process.

iCloud synchronization

iCloud synchronization is optional. When enabled, clipping information is stored in your private iCloud account using Apple CloudKit so that it can be available on your devices. Apple operates and secures the iCloud service.

Sharing and Shortcuts

Text you send to English Clippings through the Share Sheet, app actions, or Shortcuts is imported into the app and handled like a clipping you create directly.

Diagnostics

The app may produce operational diagnostic messages to help identify errors. We do not intentionally use these messages to build advertising profiles or sell personal information.

Children

English Clippings is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can investigate and remove it where applicable.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or its services change. The effective date at the top of this page will identify the latest version.
